## What changed in this implementation pass

- Added opt-in **Metro Dispatch** on the existing City. Only this ruleset has shared zone reports, a mobile-pursuit escape prerequisite, separated search/clear phases and a faster response profile. The original GTA Chase, Arcade, Tactical and ordinary destination modes remain selectable. Ten legacy runs across two seeds and five levels matched pre-edit pose, observation, reward and replay digests both with no ruleset and with explicit `legacy`.
- Controlled engine evidence: a capable unit sustained **31.6 m/s** compared with **28.0 m/s** for the SUV and closed **9.36 m** over three seconds. The dispersion is not evidence that every patrol is faster or that a route policy works across all geometry. In another controlled fixture, a real tracked pursuit escaped at tick **656**, **161.32 m** travelled and zero contacts; the same verifier rejects waiting, disabled/crippled units and a simple boundary crossing. Sampled Metro road centerline: **483/1,035** points outside coverage, **73/1,035** in overlap.
- At 10 Hz the policy receives bounded 360-degree geometric returns and tracked uncertainty. Independent visible motion measured about **8.65 m/s** of rear closure and **1.85 s** estimated TTC in the controlled approach. Hidden twin inputs diverged by **58.8 m** over **420** ticks without altering police motion in the never-visible fixture. A shared longitudinal guard prevented one front and one reverse barrier collision observed in matched unguarded runs. This does **not** solve side avoidance, unknown hazards, all pedestrian situations, or escape under rear pressure.
- The standalone CPU worker and CLI use the same Metro rollouts. The continuous PPO actor-critic uses 24 `tanh` hidden features, three squashed Gaussian action components (steer, signed pedal and brake), four clipped-surrogate epochs per rollout, 32-sample minibatches, Adam at `0.0003`, and GAE with `gamma=.99`, `lambda=.95`. Optimizer moments, counters, environment manifest identity, the sampled seed schedule and checkpoints are recorded. The held-duration limit truncates with value bootstrap; waiting still has a negative time cost. The untrained control shares the same initialization prior, not the learned weights. Reversing the reward on the same recorded inputs changes the update, and the saved/resumed update matches uninterrupted training.
- In the actual Lab, two user actions took the checkpoint from none to two updates and **120 actual transitions**. The saved file's exact SHA-256 was displayed in the original City and was used by frozen inference. Tested incompatible import rejection, explicit start, `I` and `Space` takeover, touch accelerator hold/release, and stop preserving non-pursuit objects. The guard, paused UI and source attribution remain separate from visual RGB perception. Ordinary autopilot never loads this model automatically.
